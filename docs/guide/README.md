# The NitroLearn handbook

Everything about NitroLearn, one topic per page, in plain words. The same pages open inside the dashboard:
press **Help** in its top band.

## Start here
- [Getting started](getting-started.md): what you need, installing, updating and uninstalling.
- [Your first bot](first-bot.md): make a bot, train it, stop it and carry on.
- [Reading the dashboard](dashboard.md): what every page, tab and chart tells you.
- [The training coach](coach.md): a plan in four stages and hints when training goes wrong.

## Making a better bot
- [Rewards](rewards.md): what a reward is, how to see what each one pays, and how to spot farming.
- [Learning from replays](replays.md): copy how players play before training, or while training.
- [Exploiters](exploiters.md): bots trained only to beat yours, so yours fixes its weak spots.
- [Ball prediction](ball-prediction.md): letting a bot see where the ball will be.
- [Measuring your bot](measuring.md): a report card against scripted players, a strength chart, and
  its skills and weak spots.
- [More settings](more-settings.md): bigger networks, attention for 2v2 and 3v3, team sizes, comparing
  settings, and the choices to make before a long run.

## Playing and sharing
- [Playing your bot in RLBot](rlbot.md): make its RLBot bot and play a match against it.
- [Sharing bots](sharing.md): pass a bot on, or train it on another computer.
- [Bringing a GigaLearn bot over](gigalearn.md): keep training a GigaLearn bot here as the same bot.
- [Bringing an RLGym-PPO bot over](rlgym.md): the same for a bot trained with RLGym-PPO.

## When something is wrong
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): find what you see, and what to do about it.
- [FAQ](faq.md): the questions people ask most.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): every word that sounds technical, explained.
- [All commands](commands.md): for people who like typing.
- [What was new in earlier versions](whats-new.md).

Getting help: NitroLearn is supported in the Vanta Discord server, <https://discord.gg/bYP3kYtryg>. The
dashboard's **Help** page has a **Copy a report for Discord** button that puts NitroLearn's version, what
**Check this PC** found, the bot's settings and the end of its console on the clipboard, with your user name
and folder names taken out, ready to paste into your message.
