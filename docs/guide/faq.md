# FAQ

**Is NitroLearn a cheat?**
No. It trains bots for offline play only: RLBot matches, private matches against your own bots, bot
tournaments and research. Never use a NitroLearn bot online against real players: it is cheating and breaks
Rocket League's terms of service. NitroLearn has no feature for online play and none will be added.

**Is it free?**
Free for non-commercial use. You may not sell NitroLearn or a changed copy of it. The bots you train are
yours, as long as they are never used to cheat online. `LICENSE` and `BOTS-LICENSE.md` in NitroLearn's folder
have the exact terms.

**Do I need a graphics card?**
An NVIDIA card with 6 GB or more is strongly recommended. Without one, NitroLearn still works, but training
runs on the processor and is many times slower.

**Do I need Rocket League installed to train?**
No. Training runs in a simulator, with the arena files NitroLearn downloads. You need Rocket League (and
RLBot v5) only to play your bot in a real match.

**How long until my bot is good?**
With an NVIDIA card: the first save after a few minutes, chasing and touching the ball within the first
hour, and many hours (a day or more) before it is fun to play against. The [training coach](coach.md) shows
how far it has come.

**Can I use my PC while it trains?**
Yes, for light work. Games and other programs that use the graphics card slow training down and can run the
card out of memory. Stop the bot whenever you need the PC; it carries on later exactly where it stopped.

**Does training stop when I close the dashboard?**
No. Training started from the dashboard keeps going; open the dashboard again to see it. **Stop and save**
stops it.

**Will an update break my bot?**
No. A bot keeps resuming, exporting and playing the same in every later version, and new features are off
unless you turn them on. Updates never touch your bots, saves, settings or replays.

**Which bot should I make first?**
The **1v1 starter**, with the suggested size. Its settings are the best tested.

**Should I change the rewards?**
Not for a first bot: the starters come with a proven mix. Once it plays, see [Rewards](rewards.md).

**Can my bot learn from my own replays?**
Yes: **Learn from my replays first** when you make a new bot. See [Learning from replays](replays.md).

**Can I share my bot?**
Yes. Zip its RLBot folder and pass it on; see [Sharing bots](sharing.md).

**Does NitroLearn send anything about me?**
When the dashboard opens (at most once a day) it asks GitHub for the newest version number, and sends nothing
about you; you can turn that off in App settings. Weights & Biases charts are sent only if you switch them on.
Nothing in the dashboard listens on a network port.

**Where do I get help?**
The Vanta Discord server, <https://discord.gg/bYP3kYtryg>. Paste a report from the **Help** page's **Copy a
report for Discord** button with your question.
